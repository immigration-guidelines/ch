Swiss cantons: published directives of the cantonal migration and naturalisation authorities
Checked 2026-11-15. One line per canton: what was found, and the page(s) checked. Files are listed in index.tsv; items that exist but could not be fetched are in not-obtained.tsv. Collected by download_ch_cantons.py.

ZH  Zürich: systematic. 27 "Weisungen" of the Migrationsamt (section "Weisungen" of https://www.zh.ch/de/sicherheitsdirektion/migrationsamt/formulare-broschueren-weisungen-des-migrationsamts.html) and the three editions of the Gemeindeamt's "Handbuch Einbürgerungen" (https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung.html). Further material for communes and police is behind a login (.../einbuergerung/informationen-fuer-behoerden-zur-einbuergerung.html).
BE  Bern: small but systematic. Three BSIG directives of the Migrationsdienst to the communes (https://www.migration.sid.be.ch/de/start/gemeinde-informationen/weisungen.html) and the BSIG "Wegleitung Einbürgerungsverfahren" (https://www.einbuergerung.sid.be.ch/de/start/einbuergerung/rechtliche-grundlagen.html). German versions only. The BSIG database (https://www.gemeinden.dij.be.ch/de/start/bsig/bsig-datenbank.html, a session-based JavaScript application) was not read and may hold more items; the commune platforms of both offices are login-only.
LU  Luzern: nothing systematic published. https://migration.lu.ch/formulare (11 sub-pages) and https://gemeinden.lu.ch/Einbuergerungen hold only applicant leaflets, forms and a fee overview.
UR  Uri: nothing systematic published. https://www.ur.ch/arbeit/6424, https://www.ur.ch/unterinstanzen/907, https://www.ur.ch/ausweise/1238: links to the SEM and applicant leaflets.
SZ  Schwyz: nothing systematic found, not fully checked. robots.txt of www.sz.ch shuts out AI agents by name, so only https://www.sz.ch/afm was read; web search shows applicant leaflets only (https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/...).
OW  Obwalden: a few isolated documents. Five notes of the Amt für Justiz for the communes on naturalisation, mostly under the pre-2018 law, in the publication list https://www.ow.ch/publikationen. Migration side (https://www.ow.ch/fachbereiche/1822): applicant information only.
NW  Nidwalden: nothing systematic published. https://www.nw.ch/publikationen, https://www.nw.ch/amtjustizdienste/1766, https://www.nw.ch/zsadienste/1590: applicant leaflets only.
GL  Glarus: one document. "Richtlinien / Praxis Niederlassungsbewilligung" of the Abteilung Migration (found by web search; https://www.gl.ch/public/upload/assets/44606/...). robots.txt of www.gl.ch shuts out AI agents by name, so the site was not read and the copy comes from the Wayback Machine.
ZG  Zug: nothing systematic published. https://zg.ch/de/sicherheitsdirektion/amt-fuer-migration (links to the SEM Weisungen), https://zg.ch/de/migration-integration/einreise-und-aufenthalt/aufenthalt-und-niederlassung, https://zg.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung/ordentliche-einbuergerung: forms and applicant leaflets.
FR  Fribourg: one document in two languages. Practical guide on ordinary naturalisation for the communes (https://www.fr.ch/vie-quotidienne/permis-de-sejour-et-naturalisation/la-naturalisation and the German page). The migration office SPoMi (https://www.fr.ch/dsjs/spomi) publishes no directives; its "communiqués aux communes" concern the residents' register.
SO  Solothurn: naturalisation side systematic, migration side nothing. Ten circulars and working aids of the Amt für Gemeinden for the communal naturalisation authorities (https://so.ch/verwaltung/volkswirtschaftsdepartement/amt-fuer-gemeinden/buergerrecht/informationen-rechtsgrundlagen-und-formulare/) and three chapters of the "Handbuch Einwohnerkontrolle" (.../gemeindeorganisation/publikationen/einwohnerkontrolle/). The Migrationsamt (https://so.ch/verwaltung/departement-des-innern/migrationsamt/) publishes only applicant leaflets and forms. The page "Bürgerrecht bis 31.12.2017" was not opened.
BS  Basel-Stadt: nothing systematic published. https://www.bs.ch/jsd/bdm/migrationsamt and https://www.bs.ch/themen/persoenliches-und-wohnen/einbuergerungen: forms and applicant leaflets. A "Leitfaden Einbürgerung" of the canton and the Bürgergemeinden is cited in official papers but not linked; www.bgbasel.ch answered HTTP 403.
BL  Basel-Landschaft: not verified. https://www.baselland.ch/politik-und-behorden/direktionen/sicherheitsdirektion/amt_fuer_migration answered HTTP 403 on the first request; web search shows applicant leaflets and forms only.
SH  Schaffhausen: nothing systematic found, listing not fully verified. https://sh.ch/CMS/Webseite/Kanton-Schaffhausen/Beh-rde/Verwaltung/Departement-des-Innern/Migrationsamt-und-Passb-ro-3454-DE.html loads its content by JavaScript; web search shows only applicant "Hilfsblätter" (https://sh.ch/fileadmin/Redaktoren/MuP/).
AR  Appenzell Ausserrhoden: nothing published. The pages for the residents' offices (https://ar.ch/verwaltung/departement-inneres-und-sicherheit/amt-fuer-inneres/abteilung-migration/informationen-fuer-einwohnerkontrollen/) and the naturalisation "Leitfaden" (.../abteilung-buergerrecht-und-zivilstand/einbuergerungen/leitfaden/) exist but are login-only.
AI  Appenzell Innerrhoden: nothing systematic published. https://www.ai.ch/themen/auslaender and sub-pages: forms, leaflets and a civics booklet for applicants.
SG  St. Gallen: nothing systematic published. https://www.sg.ch/sicherheit/einreise-aufenthalt-ausreise.html and https://www.sg.ch/recht/buergerrecht-zivilstand/einbuergerung.html: applicant leaflets and forms; the handbook for the naturalisation councils is in a login-only extranet (.../einbuergerungsraete-login.html). robots.txt closes /content/ (all PDFs) to scripts.
GR  Graubünden: a few isolated documents. Guidelines of the hardship commission, directives on support of persons in the asylum sector, and a guide on work assignments (https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/djsg/afm/dokumente/asyl/Seiten/default.aspx). The page "Gesetze, Verordnungen und Weisungen" (.../dienstleistungen/Einreise_Aufenthalt/Seiten/Gesetze.aspx) links only to the SEM. /DE/publikationen/Verwaltungsverordnungen/ is closed by robots.txt and was not checked.
AG  Aargau: naturalisation side systematic. Electronic handbook for the ordinary naturalisation procedure with five annexes (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/migration-integration/einbuergerung/ordentliche-einbuergerung). Migration office MIKA: of about 40 numbered Merkblätter (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/migration-integration/merkblaetter-formulare/merkblaetter) only the "Bewilligungspraxis" paper on employment of persons from the asylum sector is officer guidance; the circulars to communes are in a login-only extranet.
TG  Thurgau: nothing systematic published. https://migrationsamt.tg.ch/ (sitemap and six topic pages; the host then answered HTTP 429 and was left alone) and https://hz.tg.ch/buergerrecht.html/3220: applicant leaflets; "Dokumente für Einwohnerdienste" is login-only.
TI  Ticino: not verifiable on the site. www4.ti.ch answers scripts with a bot check (also for robots.txt). Web search points to "Direttive e circolari" of the Sezione degli enti locali to the communes; the 2009 naturalisation directives were taken from the Wayback Machine. Nothing found for the Ufficio della migrazione.
VD  Vaud: systematic, but not listable by script. The SPOP's numbered directives and circulars to the communal residents' offices and its naturalisation "directives - fiches pratiques" sit in the database of letters to the communes (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/communes/courrier-circulaire); robots.txt closes its listing (Disallow: /*&cHash=*) and the old file addresses are dead. Six items held by the Wayback Machine were saved.
VS  Valais: one document in two languages, not obtained. Guide on ordinary naturalisation for the communes (https://www.vs.ch/web/spm/download-center, https://www.vs.ch/web/spm/naturalisation-ordinaire1); robots.txt closes /documents/ and all PDFs to scripts and the Wayback Machine has no usable copy. Nothing on the aliens-law side.
NE  Neuchâtel: nothing systematic published. https://www.ne.ch/autorites/decs/smig, .../smig/ocse and https://www.ne.ch/themes/migration-et-integration/naturalisation: forms and statutes.
GE  Genève: nothing systematic published. Of the 115 OCPM publications (https://www.ge.ch/publication?organisation=6) two "synthèses" state the practice applied (integration criteria for the C permit; proof of years of residence); no OCPM item in the publication type "Directives et règlements internes".
JU  Jura: nothing systematic published. https://www.jura.ch/fr/Autorites/Administration/DSJP/SPOP/ (Police des étrangers, Naturalisations, Contrôle des habitants; sitemap searched): forms and applicant leaflets.
